Cyberspace K-12 Summit

At Slippery Rock University on NOV 5th, 2026

Cybersecurity 1✦AP Cybersecurity 2

Empowering Schools to Secure the Future.

Designed to address the growing digital defense landscape, this cybersecurity outreach initiative at Slippery Rock University organized by Center for Cybersecurity and Advanced Computing (C2AC) focuses directly on the K-12 ecosystem. Connecting regional educators, school administrators, and higher education leaders, this forum evaluates modern digital safety, network security, and classroom-ready cybersecurity curriculum. Attendees will walk away with practical, real-world defensive methodologies to build strong institutional resilience and introduce students early to critical Cybersecurity career pathways.
