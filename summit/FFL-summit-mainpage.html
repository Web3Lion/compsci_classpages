Hedera Token Studio

A teacher toolkit for the Hedera Token Service (HTS). Create and manage real cryptocurrency tokens, fungible (FT) or NFT, on Hedera testnet or mainnet so students can see how digital assets are minted, sent, and tracked on a public ledger.

  1. Open the toolkit.
  2. Create a token and set its name, symbol, and supply.
  3. Send tokens to an account and look up the transaction on the ledger.
