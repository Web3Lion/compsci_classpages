This is why the AP CSP rubric requires you to explain why a list was structurally necessary, not just show one in your code. "It would take too many variables" isn't the reason \u2014 the real reason is that with individual variables, every change to how much data you have forces you to rewrite the program. A list separates "how the program works" from "how much data it's handling," so the same 4 lines work whether N is 3 or 3 million.
