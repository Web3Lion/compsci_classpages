Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

This site hosts course pages and the Capture the Flag (CTF) arena for computer science classes taught by Robert Reasey at South Fayette School District. This policy explains what information the site collects when you sign in with Google, how it is used, and how to have it removed.

Information we collect

Most pages work without signing in. When you sign in with Google to join a class, the site receives and stores:

  • From Google: your email address and a Google account ID. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data.
  • What you enter: the class code you join and the display name (handle) you choose. Students are asked to pick a handle that is not their real name.
  • Activity in the arena: challenges completed, points, badges, streaks, items and coins, wrong guesses, hints used, time on task, and timestamps.
  • Integrity events: when a CTF page detects copying, pasting, or leaving the page during a challenge, a record of the event is logged for the teacher.
  • Refused sign-ins: if an account is not allowed to sign in, its email address, domain, and the page it came from are logged so the teacher can fix access problems.

Your browser also stores progress, settings (such as light or dark theme), and your class session in local storage on your device.

How information is used

  • To sign you in, place you in your class, and save your progress across devices.
  • To show class leaderboards, which display handles only, never email addresses.
  • To let the teacher see class progress, review integrity events, and support learning.

Information is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. The site shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics trackers.

Who can see it

Email addresses, activity, and integrity records are visible only to teacher accounts on the class dashboard. Other students see only handles and leaderboard scores.

Where it is stored

Account and class data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Sign-in is handled by Google. Pages are served by GitHub Pages and load fonts from Google Fonts. Each of these services processes data under its own privacy policy.

Guest visitors

During demonstrations, the teacher may open a class to guest Google accounts for a limited time. Guests' information is collected the same way and is removed from the class when the session is over or on request.

Students

Student records are handled under South Fayette School District policies. Students under 13 should use the site only as directed by their teacher and school.

Keeping and deleting information

Class data is kept for the school year it belongs to. When a class is deleted, its students, progress, captures, and integrity logs are removed. To have your own information removed sooner, contact the teacher below. You can clear the data stored on your device at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
